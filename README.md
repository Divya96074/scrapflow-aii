## 🚀 Live Demo
- **Deployed Application:** https://scrapflow-ai-hub.lovable.app

---

## 📌 Problem & Impact
Over 60% of recyclable industrial waste ends up in landfills due to manual sorting delays and lack of local pricing transparency. **ScrapFlow AI** leverages automated vision triage and real-time logistics telemetry to streamline material circularity.

---

## ✨ Key Features
- 🤖 **AI Waste Scanner:** Computer-vision inspection for plastic, e-waste, and scrap metal purity scoring.
- 🛒 **Scrap Marketplace:** Verified listings connecting material producers directly with regional recyclers.
- 🚚 **Logistics Fleet Map:** Fill-level sensors prioritizing high-urgency pickup bins to minimize collection emissions.
- 📊 **ESG Metrics Engine:** Real-time CO2 emissions saved and diversion tonnage counters.
